Philippine Literacy Statistics

  • PSA 2024 FLEMMS: Found a 93.1% basic literacy rate among Filipinos aged 10–64 compared to a 70.8% functional literacy rate.
  • World Bank Learning Poverty Data: Estimated that 91% of Filipino children at late primary age struggle with reading proficiency.

Why We Made This App

  • To give students an easy place to look up any book, pick up story and writing-craft notes right inside the book card, and build steady reading habits.
  • To connect reading with practical study tools, local Hinigaran reading spots, and Hinigaran National High School’s school publications (The Voice and Ang Tinig).

        Why School Publications & Journalists Matter

        • Accurate campus information: School publications keep students, teachers, and families informed about school events, policies, and community happenings.
        • Student voice and accountability: Student journalists highlight real student concerns, creative work, and everyday stories that might otherwise go unheard.
        • Fact-checking and clear writing: Reporting teaches students to verify details, ask careful questions, and write clearly in both English and Filipino.
        • Civic habits early on: Practicing fair, responsible journalism builds critical thinking and active citizenship that last beyond high school.
