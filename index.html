Regular reading sharpens vocabulary, attention span, and critical thinking across subjects. Here is why reading matters and why we built Quillfinite:

Philippine Literacy & Reading Context
  • PSA 2024 FLEMMS: Reports 93.1% basic literacy and 70.8% functional literacy among Filipinos aged 10–64.
  • Reading Comprehension Gap: World Bank & international assessments show many upper-elementary students still struggle with reading proficiency.
  • Everyday Impact: Strong reading habits help students unpack textbooks, research articles, and word problems faster.
Why We Made Quillfinite
  • Works Beyond Fixed Lists: Pulls records for any book via Open Library and Google Books instead of a hardcoded shelf.
  • Craft Before You Commit: Shares writing-craft notes right inside each scanned book so students see how scenes and arguments work.
  • Built for Student Study: Combines ready-to-copy citations, essay angles, and local Hinigaran reading spots in one place.

Why School Publications & Student Journalists Matter

  • Truth & Accountability: Student journalists document school events, policies, and campus concerns with fairness and fact-checking.
  • Amplifying Student Perspectives: School publications give learners across grade levels a space to share lived experiences in both English and Filipino.
  • Critical Thinking & Civic Habits: Reporting, interviewing, and editing train students to verify sources before sharing information online.
  • Preserving Campus Memory: Every issue records milestones, achievements, and everyday moments that shape Hinigaran National High School's history.
