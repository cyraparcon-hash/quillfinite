        The Voice & Ang Tinig — student journalism, voice, and why school publications matter.

        The Voice

        English

        The Voice is the English school publication of Hinigaran National High School, highlighting campus stories, student perspectives, creative writing, and community milestones.

        Visit The Voice Facebook Page ↗

        Ang Tinig

        Filipino

        Ang Tinig is the Filipino school publication of Hinigaran National High School, giving space to student voices, cultural stories, essays, and everyday life in our own language.

        Visit Ang Tinig Facebook Page ↗

        Why It Is Important to Have School Publications

        • • Gives students a real voice: School publications let learners share ideas, questions, creative works, and experiences that matter to the student body.
        • • Builds truth, critical thinking, and care: Reporting school events and stories teaches careful listening, fact-checking, empathy, and responsible expression.
        • • Preserves school memory and culture: Every issue records milestones, traditions, and student perspectives—especially in both English and Filipino—so future students can see what shaped their school community.
